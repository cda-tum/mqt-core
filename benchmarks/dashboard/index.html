    What is measured?

    Compilation time

    Copy the generated Core program, convert QC → QCO, compile for the target, convert to native QC, and dispose of the result. Circuit generation and quality analysis are outside the timer.

    Circuit quality

    Two-qubit gates are counted after mapping and synthesis. Depth counts U/CX gate layers; terminal measurements add no depth and barriers synchronize wires.

    Comparison conditions

    GHZ, QFT, and static Bernstein–Vazirani come from mqt.core.bench. Compare the same environment and benchmark suite. Generator changes can change inputs; mapping trials depend on available CPUs.
